# HAZARA rules.v1

Source: Pagat Hazari (Bangladesh). Bhutan Hazarey is not part of v1.

Four people. One standard 52-card pack. Each person receives 13 cards and splits them into sets of 3, 3, 3, and 4. Sets must be weakly descending: each set is equal to or stronger than the next. The 4-card set is judged by its best 3-card subset, which must be the weakest set. The spare card still scores if that set is captured.

## Rank and points

Ranks, high to low: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2.

A, K, Q, J, and 10 are worth 10 points. 2 through 9 are worth 5. A deal totals 360.

Combination strength decides who captures. Points are the captured cards.

## Combinations, strongest first

1. **Troy** — three of the same rank. Aces beat kings, down to twos.
2. **Colour Run** — three consecutive cards of one suit. A–K–Q is highest, then A–2–3, then K–Q–J, down to 4–3–2.
3. **Run** — three consecutive ranks, not all one suit. Same order as Colour Run.
4. **Colour** — three of one suit that are not a run. Compare the highest card, then the next, then the lowest.
5. **Pair** — two of one rank plus a side card. Compare the pair, then the side card.
6. **Indi** — none of the above. Compare highest, then next, then lowest.

Ace joins a run with the king or with the 2, and not with both. 2–A–K of one suit is Colour, not a Colour Run.

Equal combinations: the later player in that comparison’s play order wins. Suits do not break a tie beyond defining Colour and Colour Run.

## Play

Deal and play are anticlockwise. The first comparison is led by the player right of the dealer. Each person plays their current strongest remaining set. The winner leads the next comparison. The fourth comparison is the 4-card sets (16 cards).
